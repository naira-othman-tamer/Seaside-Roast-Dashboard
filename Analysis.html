Ad Campaign Performance Dashboard

Insights & Recommendations

  • Loyal customers generate the highest revenue and conversions — maintain loyalty programs and personalized offers.
  • Revenue peaks between December and March — plan seasonal promotions and allocate more ad spend during this period.
  • February and March show growth without matching efficiency — refine targeting and optimize conversion strategy.
  • New customers display high conversion value — enhance onboarding campaigns to convert them into loyal clients.
  • Use weather-based promotions (e.g., discounts on rainy days) to sustain engagement during low-tourist seasons.
